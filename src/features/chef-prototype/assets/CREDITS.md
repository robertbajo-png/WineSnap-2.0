# Prototype Image Sources

These are illustrative culinary images, not evidence of actual cooking history,
ingredient identification, nutrition or a particular purchased wine.

## Generated Images

`cauliflower.webp` and `salad.webp` were created with the built-in image generator
on 2026-10-20, then resized/compressed with Sharp. Original generated files remain
in the operator's generated-image directory; the actual consumed WebP assets are
bundled in this repository. No private user images were submitted.

Cauliflower prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: original food photograph for a working personal cookbook application. Generate one wide landscape editorial food photograph, no UI, no text. Subject: a generous shallow white ceramic bowl of golden roasted cauliflower florets on silky white bean puree, with bright green parsley, a little olive oil and a clearly visible grilled lemon half. Delicious honest home cooking, edible textures, natural imperfections. Bowl positioned in the right two-thirds of the composition, seen at a 45 degree overhead angle; entire bowl visible with breathing room. On the left, naturally dark forest-green linen on a kitchen table with generous quiet negative space where white UI text will later be overlaid. On the right soft daylight reveals the food beautifully, no artificial dramatic darkness on the dish. Palette of vibrant leafy green, lemon yellow, neutral white, natural golden cauliflower, subtly dark green textile. High-end contemporary cookbook photography, crisp sharp detail. A small fork and a folded linen cloth may be present but no hands, no wine bottles, no lettering, no watermarks, no border, no collage.

Salad prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: culinary photograph for a personal cookbook recipe. A crisp daylight editorial photograph of a fresh avocado, cucumber and leafy green salad with lemon olive oil dressing in a simple white ceramic bowl, a lemon wedge alongside. Clearly visible ripe avocado slices and cucumber chunks, tender salad leaves; no additional ingredients. Overhead three-quarter angle, entire bowl in frame with some breathing room. Clean neutral pale gray tabletop, small folded sage green linen napkin. Bright fresh natural colors, appetizing and believable home cooking, sharp detail and texture. Horizontal landscape composition, no text, no watermark, no UI, no collage.
