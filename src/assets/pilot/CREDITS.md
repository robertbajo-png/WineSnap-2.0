# Pilot Food And Wine Image

`food-wine.webp` is an original image generated with the image-generation tool on
2026-11-05, then resized to 1440px wide and encoded as WebP with Sharp. The original
generated PNG is retained outside the repository. No private images were inputs.

Prompt: a crisp natural-daylight landscape editorial photograph of tomato and
basil pasta on a white plate beside a clear glass of white wine, with both food
and wine visible on a neutral charcoal-green tabletop. No people, brand, text,
UI, collage, artificial lighting effects or claims about a particular wine.

This is illustrative imagery, not a photograph of the user's meal or a verified
wine identity, pairing, ingredients, nutrition or ownership. It is a separate
asset, not a crop of the raster UI design boards.
